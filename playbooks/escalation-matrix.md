# Escalation matrix

## Severity rules

- Critical: confirmed active compromise, broad lateral movement, ransomware behavior, or privileged account misuse.
- High: suspicious execution with strong evidence, credential theft indicators, or high-criticality endpoint impact.
- Medium: credible signal that needs owner confirmation, identity review, or endpoint follow-up.
- Low: weak signal, expected admin activity, or benign activity with a clear business explanation.

## Owner rules

- Endpoint activity: endpoint operations plus security analyst.
- Identity activity: identity administrator plus security analyst.
- Finance or privileged user impact: security lead review before closure.
- Any public exposure or customer data risk: legal and compliance review before closure.

## Evidence required before closure

- Alert ID and time window.
- Affected user and device.
- Severity and reason.
- Containment decision.
- Evidence captured.
- Remaining risk.
- Follow-up owner.
